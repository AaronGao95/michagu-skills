## Selection and variation

- Choose references by shot type and product fit: packaging hero, tea-making action, tea vessel detail, outdoor scene, or environmental wide shot.
- For composition, transfer camera angle, crop, subject placement, focus hierarchy, light direction, and shadow structure. Change the specific surface, supporting prop, and surrounding context so output is a new plausible photograph rather than a near-copy.
- Do not combine incompatible lighting schemes (e.g. hard direct sun plus shadowless high-key fill) unless the chosen real reference visibly does so.
- Give the generation tool the selected local scene reference(s) alongside the user’s product photo whenever supported. The product image governs package identity; the scene image governs photographic treatment.

# Style menu for each new product image

When a product image arrives without a style choice, ask the user to choose one of these looks. These are photographic looks derived from all 26 images, not brand-design presets. They can request one by number or combine a main look with a specific camera/light preference.

1. **明亮白桌·柔光细节** — High-key white/light-gray tabletop; soft window light and low-contrast shadows; sparse props; top-oblique or near-overhead close/medium detail. Product and tea objects read cleanly; background stays quiet. Strong matches: `崎寻/龙井春茶｜充沛的鲜能量_1...jpg`, `_2...jpg`, `_5...jpg`, `福利更新｜次方杯...jpg`, `柔和而明亮的香气...jpg`.
2. **白桌·硬光几何影** — Pale tabletop with directional sunlight, crisp cast shadows, abundant negative space, often overhead or high-oblique still life. Focus on object arrangement and shadow shape. Strong matches: `BASAO/一个理想的秋日下午_2...jpg`, `BASAO/流动中安静存在...jpg`, `BASAO/当下自然发生...jpg`.
3. **暖暗茶席·局部光** — Dark wood/counter, low-key exposure, warm side light illuminating hands, cup, or brewing action; background falls into deep shadow. Tight or medium close framing; controlled focal point. Strong matches: `BASAO/BASAO selection｜Yumiko 侧把壶套组_1...jpg`, `_2...jpg`, `BASAO｜城市茶空间...jpg`, `一个理想的秋日下午_1...jpg`.
4. **林间·斑驳逆光散景** — Outdoor rock, wood, or tea table; deep green foliage; small product or cup against a softened background; back/side sunlight creates irregular leaf bokeh and patches of light. Eye-level or low close/medium shot; subject sharp, background distinctly softer. Strong matches: `崎寻/⛰️展开一段山野时间...jpg`, `产地寻游｜岩谷的幽长兰香...jpg`, `季节的气息_1...jpg`, `_2...jpg`, `_3...jpg`.
5. **茶空间·建筑光影** — Wide or medium view with timber, windows, chairs, tables, and plants; sun stripes or window shadows define room geometry; more environment is visible and focus is deeper than in a close product portrait. Strong matches: `崎寻/在山海连接处，风味抵达_1...jpg`, `_3...jpg`.
6. **临水·清淡留白** — Pale blue/gray water or sky horizon, natural light, simple table edge or rough shoreline foreground, sparse product-and-cup arrangement, medium framing, airy negative space. Strong matches: `BASAO/一个理想的秋日下午_1...jpg`, `BASAO/夏日饮茶 品味依然_2...jpg`.

Shared palette and photographic traits: daylight rather than colored studio lighting; restrained cream, wood, gray, green, and tea-amber tones; real surfaces and contact shadows; compositions range from sparse overhead still lifes to hand-held/brewing details and architectural wides. Depth of field is not a single house style: bright tabletop examples can keep several objects legible, while forest and dark-action examples isolate one focal plane. Match the selected photo's depth rather than applying blur by default.
